What some people call the “Harry Potter bookstore” is the Lello Bookstore (Livraria Lello), located in the historic center of Porto, Portugal, is very beautiful, but:
- We had to wait ~20/30min to get it
- And it's pretty small. I would say around ~350 square meters, divided into 2 floors
